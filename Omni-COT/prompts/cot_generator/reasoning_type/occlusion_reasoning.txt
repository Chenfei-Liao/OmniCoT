**Reasoning Task: Wall-Occlusion Analysis (Type 4)**
Determine whether two objects can see each other by checking wall barriers.

**Reasoning Steps Required (2-4 steps):**
1. Identify object positions using natural language.
2. Determine their relative spatial arrangement.
3. Check for potential wall barriers.
4. Consider window transparency when relevant.
5. Conclude with "Yes" or "No".
