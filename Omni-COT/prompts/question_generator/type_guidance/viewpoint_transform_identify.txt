    **Viewpoint-Identify Guidelines:**
    - Start from SPECIFIC object in scene
    - Initial facing: cardinal direction OR another object
    - 2 sequential rotations (e.g., "turn 90° right, then 45° left")
    - End with: "what is the NEAREST object?"

    **Example:**
    "Standing at the stool next to the chair, facing north toward the table, turn 90° clockwise, then turn 45° counterclockwise. What is the NEAREST object directly ahead?"
